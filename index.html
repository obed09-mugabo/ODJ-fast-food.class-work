About ODJ Fast Food

ODJ Fast Food is a modern quick-service restaurant. We cook burgers, pizzas and fries from fresh ingredients, and we serve them with sodas and bottled water in a clean, bright dining room.

Our promise is simple: hot food, friendly service and no long waits. Eat in, take away or order for delivery.
